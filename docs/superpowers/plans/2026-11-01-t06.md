## Global Constraints

Pure model separate from market; P2/unadopted/BET disabled; UTC storage/Beijing display; no overwriting reports or production data. Explicit historical replay. No website, automated bets or fabricated validation.

## Review Focus

- Already-started cached fixtures excluded at forecast cutoff.
- Future-trained model never applied at an earlier forecast cutoff.
- Market snapshots complete and contemporaneous; model independent when quotes absent.
- Frozen exports stable after scores/odds change; failed commit leaves no fake completed report.
- Missing IDs, leagues or teams preserved as exclusions, not guessed or silently dropped.
