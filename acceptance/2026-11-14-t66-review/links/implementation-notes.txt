Architecture
- FD import creates/updates its normal result row, then adds only
  existing_apif.counterpart_id -> fd.match_id in the same row savepoint.
- APIF Match team IDs, status, kickoff, result provider/check time, Result scores,
  availability, and Team aliases remain untouched. No global team inference.
- Exact canonical oriented IDs, or exactly one apif:team:<positive numeric ID>
  with the other canonical side equal, are the only accepted identity forms.
  Same league and <=3h are required. Shared teams veto competing/reversed
  candidates in both directions; all incoming FD rows are considered before
  any row can win by order. Unrelated external claims remain a veto.
- One verified FDORG sibling may coexist with the APIF link on the FD anchor.
  Existing late-FDORG import and relink restrictions stay in place for other claims.
- Existing APIF -> FD observations recheck the full stored relation and current
  provider observation. A new partial APIF observation without that pointer still
  follows the original quarantine path.
- Known APIF provider bindings require one owner across the entire Team table.
  An opaque side cannot contradict a scoped provider binding on another team;
  a unique binding on the actual FD opposite side is consistent and does not
  rewrite the old opaque APIF row. Other-league bindings do not establish identity.
- Unmapped FD raw-name opposition vs an already canonical APIF opponent retains
  original FD admission without a guessed relation. This preserves the two such
  USA cases; five other USA cases are genuine one-opaque-APIF candidates.
